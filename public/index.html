Why it works

Most players plateau not from lack of technique — from the mental side.

We address that directly — with a mental coach, structured workshops, video review, and daily practices that build real habits you will use in your next match.

And we do all of this from one of the last untouched stretches of Atlantic coastline in Europe. You sleep in Milfontes every night. You unpack once. By Tuesday it already feels like home.

The differentiator

The mental game.
What no one else addresses.

01
Foundation
Awareness

Noticing what actually happens in your mind under pressure — not what you think should happen. Video review on Thursday is built specifically for this. Most players have never honestly observed themselves.

02
The skill
Process

Your personal 10-second pre-point routine — a physical trigger that redirects attention from the last error to the next point. You build it on Day 2, test it all week, and trust it on Saturday.

03
The practice
Release

Yoga, breathwork, and sound therapy train the nervous system to let go faster. Players who release faster recover faster. The morning ritual is not optional wellness — it is the foundation of every day.

Every Swiss golf club runs travel weeks abroad. Members go with their coach, play somewhere beautiful, come back better players and more loyal to the club. We built exactly that for padel — in one of the most beautiful places in Europe.

What you already know
