    Before you book

    Four things
    worth knowing.

    Cancellation

    Cancelation 15 days before is possible but we would love to have you in another possible date.

    Minimum numbers

    An edition confirms at 8 players. If we don't reach that, we tell you 15 days ahead and refund in full — or you move to the next edition, your choice.

    Playing level

    From a confident club player upward. Seven days is long enough that a large gap in level shows, so tell us where you play and we will say honestly whether the edition suits you.

    What language

    The week is run in English, with German spoken throughout. Portuguese where you want it. The table finds its own language by Tuesday.

    What is not in the price

    Flights, personal travel insurance, and drinks outside the meals listed in your package. Everything else on the programme is included.

    Payment

    The full amount is paid at booking. Your place is confirmed the moment the payment clears — there is no balance to chase later and nothing further to pay before you arrive.
